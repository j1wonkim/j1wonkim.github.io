I’m a political scientist focused on translating hard-to-measure human attitudes, political behavior, and government rhetoric into data and models that explain and predict real-world outcomes.

My academic research spans human rights, democracy, and public opinion. My work has been published in peer-reviewed journals, supported by research grants, and recognized with a paper award.

I am an incoming Adjunct Instructor at the Oxford College of Emory University. Previously, I was a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Data and Decision Sciences and a Research Faculty Fellow at the [Center of AI Learning](https://ailearning.emory.edu/) at Emory University, where I partnered with NGOs to design research, inform strategic decision-making, and strengthen their data systems.

I hold a Ph.D. and M.A. in Political Science from Emory University, and a B.A. in English literature and linguistics from Sungkyunkwan University.
